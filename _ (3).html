About

Hello, I'm . I used to love photography. Back when I was working, I took short trips whenever I could, mostly on my own and mostly close to home in Thailand, especially in Bangkok.

Slow travel by bus and train

My favourite way to get around is by city bus and by the electric trains (BTS and MRT). Riding them is part of the fun: you see streets, markets and neighbourhoods that you would miss from a car. Many of these photos began with a bus window or a station exit and a little curiosity.

What you'll find here

  • Nature: views, landscapes and flowers
  • Architecture: buildings, temples and old houses
  • Macro: close-ups of flowers and insects
  • Traveling: places in Thailand and abroad
  • Food: good meals found along the way

Why I'm sharing them

These photos have been sitting on my drives for years. I'd rather see them used, so anyone is welcome to take them for free. Please credit the photo, and get in touch first if you want to use one commercially.
